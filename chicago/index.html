Keeping Your Chicago HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of the year.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement.
