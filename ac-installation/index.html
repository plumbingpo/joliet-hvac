The Benefits of Professional AC Installation

The worst installation mistakes are the ones you cannot see at startup. Skip a proper vacuum, and moisture left in the line set turns to acid that attacks the compressor over the next few summers. Guess at the refrigerant charge instead of weighing it in, and the system wastes efficiency every hour it runs. Pick the size by rule of thumb, and an oversized unit short-cycles toward an early failure while the house stays humid. Each of these problems surfaces years later as the very repair bills and high energy costs a new system was supposed to end.

Doing it right comes down to steps most people never see. We pressure-test every line set with nitrogen, pull a vacuum and confirm it with a micron gauge, weigh in the refrigerant and double-check it using superheat and subcooling, trap the condensate drain and add overflow protection, and measure static pressure to confirm airflow instead of guessing by hand. Handling refrigerant is regulated at the federal level and calls for specialized recovery and evacuation equipment. Get all of it right, and the new system fades into the background for fifteen years of reliable cold air, which is the goal behind every job we commission.

AC Installation Prevention Tips

Smart maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Hose off the outdoor unit occasionally and keep plants away from every side
  • Fix minor problems early, since a weak capacitor found in spring will not strand you in summer
  • Avoid huge thermostat setbacks, which force long, hard runs during the hottest hours
  • Do not close vents in unused rooms; the system's airflow was balanced with all of them open
  • Change the filter on schedule from day one so the new coil never sees starved airflow
  • Schedule a spring tune-up each year to confirm the charge and electrical readings are still on spec
  • Flush the condensate line each spring so the float switch never has to save you
